01 — about me

Get to know Jacey

I am Jacey Chen B. Aguirre, a BSIT-MWA student at National University Manila, a Notion Campus Leader, and the President of the NU Wizards' Circle. I believe growth happens when you grab every opportunity that comes your way.

Still very much a beginner and proud of it, I approach tech, leadership, and community with curiosity and a willingness to learn. I engage audiences as a student host, work to bridge student needs with institutional action through campus organizations, and guide fellow members as head of the Wizards' Circle.

As a member of the NU Percussion Department, I bring the same discipline and teamwork required in tech to music and school spirit. I am on a journey of growth, continuously learning through every role I take on, and I invite others to step forward, build confidence, and create their own opportunities alongside me.
